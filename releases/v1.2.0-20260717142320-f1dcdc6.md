# Podhoc v1.2.0 — Private-document imports, Podhoc Digest, and a major reliability release

_Released 17 July 2026_

This is Podhoc's largest release since launch — new import sources, a brand-new audio digest product, and a deep security & reliability pass across the whole platform.

## ✨ New

### Import privately shared documents — Google Drive & OneDrive

- Connect your **Google Drive** or **Microsoft OneDrive** account under _Settings → Integrations_ and turn privately shared documents into podcasts.
- Pick Drive files with the built-in file picker on the web app, or simply paste a privately shared link when creating a podcast.
- A new **link pre-check** tells you _before_ generating whether Podhoc can actually read the document — no more wasted generations on inaccessible links.
- Scanned or image-only PDFs now convert too, via an OCR fallback (with a generous daily fair-use limit).

### Podhoc Digest — curated audio briefings, delivered by email

- Subscribe at **digest.podhoc.com**: pick from **10 topics** — tech, AI, climate, finance, health, science, crypto & web3, world affairs, startups, and culture — each fed by hand-verified, quality sources.
- Every month, Podhoc curates the best recent articles per topic and generates a listenable audio edition, delivered straight to your inbox with one-tap listen links.
- **Free** subscriptions get concise editions; **Premium (€3/month)** unlocks extended, more in-depth editions.
- Signups are open now — first editions arrive with the next monthly cycle.

### Stay signed in

- Web sessions now **renew silently in the background** — no more surprise logouts in the middle of a session. Sign-ins last as long as your account's session horizon allows.

## 🔐 Security & reliability

- Dependency upgrades across the backend and all web apps, resolving published security advisories.
- Hardened handling of user-supplied links and uploaded files end to end.
- Stronger privacy in operational logs and expanded audit logging.
- Broader infrastructure monitoring and alerting for faster incident detection.
- Database connection stability fixes that eliminate a class of errors under load.
- Background processing is more resilient: failed items now retry or fail cleanly (with automatic credit refunds where a generation can't complete) instead of stalling silently.

## 🎧 Creation & player improvements

- Smarter generation defaults tailored to your plan tier.
- Download your finished episodes directly from _My Podcasts_.
- Reused scripts now still receive fresh titles and descriptions.
- Failed transcript fetches are retried properly instead of caching the failure.

## ⌚ Wearables & apps

- **Apple Watch**: the standalone watch app reached App Store submission readiness, with background-audio playback.
- **Garmin**: continued improvements to the Connect IQ companion experience.

## 🌍 Website

- Marketing site refresh: updated roadmap, wearables announcements, and richer structured data so search engines and AI assistants understand Podhoc better.

## Under the hood

- More resilient deployment pipeline, expanded automated test coverage, and faster, smarter CI.

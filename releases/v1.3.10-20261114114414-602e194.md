# Podhoc 1.3.10 — more reliable listening and sign-in

This release improves mobile listening, session restoration and analytics choices. Mobile changes require the updated app to pass Apple or Google review and become available in your store. Deploying the website and backend does not update an installed app.

## Listening in the car

Android Auto can load your library when Podhoc is not already open on the phone. Browsing provides clearer loading, signed-out, empty and error states. Playback information and controls reflect the active audio player, with more reliable command delivery when the app is in the background.

## Staying signed in

Android session restoration is more reliable when changing language or recreating the app interface. Secure credential storage remains encrypted, and an outdated sign-in callback no longer clears a newer valid session. Signing out also cancels pending playback work so it cannot carry into another account.

## Layout and accessibility of controls

iOS navigation headers and safe-area spacing have been corrected, including Arabic layouts and supported folding-screen postures. Titles and controls fit more reliably around screen edges and changes in available space.

## Clearer analytics choices

The optional mobile analytics explanation describes the identifiers sent and Google's processing. Earlier approvals require renewed acceptance of the updated disclosure; refusing analytics remains respected. Turning analytics off discards queued analytics and prevents unsent batches from being sent. The server-side analytics proxy now explicitly denies advertising user-data consent and ad personalization for events it forwards.

For details about data handling, see the [privacy policy](https://podhoc.com/privacy/).
